# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participating in this project to be free of harassment for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people. A review comment on a pull request
  is about the change, not the person who made it.
- Assume the other person has context you do not. Many issues here come from a
  specific Kubernetes or OpenLDAP setup that is not visible in the report.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change. Scope decisions for this project
  are documented in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) — OpenLDAP only, and
  configuration rather than runtime state.
- Give credit. If someone's report led to a fix, say so in the changelog.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information, including email addresses, without
  explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commits — and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at the address in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are read and handled privately. The
maintainer will respond within a reasonable time and will explain any action
taken, without disclosing the reporter's identity.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that violate this code, and
may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behaviour they judge
inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
